Booking — Option 1: "Calendly three-pane card"

Channeling: Calendly. The whole flow lives in one centered card with Calendly's exact three-pane anatomy: left = who/what/how-long context rail (always visible, so patients never lose track of what they're booking), center = month calendar, right = scrollable time list for the selected day. Borrowed specifically: the pane layout; the ringed "has availability" day markers; and Calendly's signature split-button confirm — clicking a time splits it into [time | Next →] inline, killing a whole modal step. Confirm is then a second view of the same card (left rail persists, panes 2–3 swap for the details form). Tradeoff: best-in-class for a single known provider+service; the provider/service pick still happens before this card (kept as today's step), so it's a 2-scene flow, and the three panes need a thoughtful mobile collapse (Calendly stacks them: context → calendar → times).
