Calendar — Option C: "Notion-style Week Board + Detail Rail"

Direction: Notion Calendar's take on a week: seven light columns of stacked appointment cards in time order (no hour ruler, no positioned blocks) plus a persistent right-hand detail rail. Clicking a card selects it and the rail shows full info + all status actions — actions leave the list rows entirely, so rows stay one-line dense. Google Calendar remains the throughline (Today/chevrons toolbar, week framing, status colors) but the interaction model is Notion's select-then-act. Borrowed from Notion Calendar: stacked-card week columns, right detail panel, keyboard-friendly selection, quiet typography. Tradeoff: medium lift — a new layout and a selection model, but no time-positioning math; whole week visible at once (unlike A's scroll or B's one-day agenda), yet gap/duration is not spatial. Cancel confirmation happens inline in the rail (shown below) rather than a modal.
