Channeling: Zocdoc. Tapping any slot chip goes straight to a single confirm page. Borrowed specifically: Zocdoc's card-with-availability-grid search result, "Next available:" fallback line for booked-out providers, and its one-tap slot → review-and-confirm jump (fewest taps of the three options: 3 taps to booked). Tradeoff: heaviest data change — one screen needs slots for all providers at once (N slot queries or a new batch endpoint); shines with 5+ providers, looks sparse with 2. Best fit for "I want the soonest appointment, whoever it's with".
