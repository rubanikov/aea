Booking — Option 3: "Square Appointments stepper + summary rail"

Channeling: Square Appointments (its hosted online-booking site). A guided 4-step wizard — 1 Service → 2 Provider → 3 Date & time → 4 Confirm — with Square's two signature moves: services as full-width descriptive rows (name, duration, description line; scannable like a menu, room for future price/insurance info) and a persistent right summary rail that fills in as you go, each line with its own "edit" link so any step is revisitable without losing later choices. Step 2 offers Square's "Any available provider" tile, which auto-picks whoever has the soonest opening. Borrowed specifically: the step list, the accumulate-in-rail model, per-line edit links, "Any provider" fast path. Tradeoff: the most steps/ceremony of the three options — deliberate and reassuring (good for healthcare, least likely to mis-book) but slowest to a slot; the rail collapses to a sticky bottom summary bar on mobile.
