# Project Audit — 2026-08-13 16:19

Audit of the codebase against `project.md` (Patient Appointment & Scheduling Portal spec). Findings gathered via three parallel codebase-research passes covering (1) core booking flows & concurrency, (2) security/RBAC/PHI/compliance/code quality, and (3) performance/stretch features/docs/submission requirements.

## Core Functional Requirements (30 pts)
- ✅ **Patient registration & auth** — bcrypt hashing, 15-min JWT in httpOnly cookies, unauth requests globally rejected (`backend/config/settings.py:198-260`)
- ✅ **Provider availability management** — working hours + slot length + blocked time all fold into slot generation, past times excluded (`backend/scheduling/slots.py`)
- ✅ **Slot discovery & booking** — slots computed live (never stale), booking persists + audits (`backend/bookings/services.py:175-233`)
- ✅ **No double-booking under concurrency** — row lock **and** DB unique constraint, real 10-thread concurrent test proving exactly 1 success (`backend/bookings/tests/test_concurrency.py`)
- ✅ **Reschedule & cancel** — atomic old-slot-free/new-slot-book, server-side 24h notice rule (`backend/bookings/services.py:236-334`)
- ✅ **Appointment status lifecycle** — explicit transition table, invalid transitions rejected, every change audit-logged (`backend/bookings/transitions.py`)
- ✅ **Automated email reminders** — Resend-based, DB-unique-constraint dedup, every attempt logged (`backend/reminders/services.py`)

## Edge Cases & Failure Modes
- ✅ Time zones & DST (zoneinfo-based, UTC storage)
- ✅ Concurrent booking on last slot (same as Core #4)
- ✅ Provider edit colliding with a booked slot — rejected with 409 + affected list, never silent deletion
- ✅ Reminder idempotency under retry/crash
- ✅ Double-submit of booking — idempotency-key column + unique constraint + concurrent test
- ✅ Cancel/no-show transition rules
- ⚠️ **Graceful degradation & input validation** — not independently audited this pass; worth a follow-up check

## No Double-Booking Under Concurrency (20 pts)
- ✅ Provably correct under load, committed automated test — this is the strongest area of the build

## Security, Privacy & Compliance (20 pts)

- ✅ Server-side RBAC (patient/provider/admin), deny-by-default, admin bypass itself audit-logged
- ⚠️ **Encryption** — TLS enforced in app config, but at-rest encryption is unverifiable because **no infrastructure has actually been deployed**
- ✅ Append-only audit log (actor/action/target/timestamp) on every booking change
- ✅ Retention/deletion policy documented + real soft-delete account-deletion endpoint
- ✅ No PHI observed in log calls (sampled across views/services)
- ✅ Secure auth (bcrypt, httpOnly/secure cookies, rate-limited login, fail-closed secret key)
- ✅ BAA disclosure naming Resend/Railway, PHI kept out of reminder bodies

**Bottom line:** the engineering core — booking, concurrency, RBAC, audit logging, reminders, CI — is genuinely strong and would score well on rubric weight (30+20+20+15 = 85 of the 100 pts are mostly earned). Those three are explicit, non-negotiable submission requirements — worth closing before calling this done.
